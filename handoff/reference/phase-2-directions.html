Phase 2 — Brand direction exploration

Four directions — each a different metaphor for what DataBridge is.

You asked to stand out. So none of these live in the Linear / Stripe / Vercel neighborhood. Each proposes a different metaphor for the product — a workbench, an observatory, a cartographer's desk, a laboratory — and the metaphor drives type, color, tone, and detail.

All four are light-first, trustworthy, premium, tinted-neutral, and designed for the least-technical user. The same DataBridge surfaces — wordmark, KPI card, navigation, AI message, buttons — repeat in each so you can compare like for like.

Your brief, summarized:
  • Calm + quietly competent · Serious + trustworthy · Premium
  • Emotional job: data superpowers; master of your company's data
  • AI is subtle — a shimmer, not a mascot
  • Avoid: blue-SaaS, enterprise-BI, AI-slop purple, dark-hacker, cluttered
  • Light-first · Tinted neutrals · Balanced density · Boldness ≈ 60
01 / 04 · METAPHOR · THE WORKBENCH

Atelier

"A craftsman's workbench, not a SaaS app."

Warm paper surface, Fraunces serif paired with Space Grotesk, one muted terra accent that's earthy not corporate. The whole UI feels like a leather-bound journal — considered, tactile, quiet. This is the strongest "craftsman with superpowers" direction: you are not a user, you are a master at your bench.

02 / 04 · METAPHOR · THE OBSERVATORY

Observatory

"You are looking at your company from above."

Cool tinted off-white (the faintest pewter-cyan), Source Serif paired with Inter, one deep ocean accent. Data visualization is the main character — charts are framed like specimens, KPIs like readings on an instrument. Premium, calm, serious. More restrained than Atelier but with the same "this is for grownups" confidence.

03 / 04 · METAPHOR · THE CARTOGRAPHER

Cartographer

"Mapping what your company knows."

Warm ivory paper, Newsreader (contemporary serif) paired with Inter, brass-and-ink accent palette. Subtle dot grids live inside cards, echoing map plotting paper. Feels like an old-world explorer's desk — compass, ledger, plotted coordinates — but rendered with clean modern typography. Deeply on-brief: "the master of your data" is literally a cartographer.

04 / 04 · METAPHOR · THE INSTRUMENT

Laboratory

"A precision instrument for your business."

Tinted-cool white (faint sage-green), Source Serif for numbers paired with IBM Plex Sans for UI. Single deep teal accent. Near-zero corner radius, thin rules, fixed gutters. Every number is tabular; every KPI feels like a reading on a lab instrument. Restrained but sharp. More architectural than Observatory, more utilitarian than Atelier.
