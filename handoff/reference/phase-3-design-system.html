Observatory.

"You are looking at your company from above."

A quiet, premium, light-first design system for DataBridge. Built from one conviction: the product should make someone feel like the master of their data — not a visitor to it. Serif for authority and voice; sans for every surface you touch; mono for every coordinate, label, and piece of technical truth. One accent color (deep ocean) earns its place. Every other color has a clear, single job.

Color.

All neutrals share a cool pewter tint (≈210° hue, ≈5% chroma). The eye reads them as neutral — the feeling is of a calm, slightly-cool room. One accent (deep ocean) does all the heavy lifting for primary actions. State colors are muted variants chosen to coexist with the neutrals, never to shout.

06 / 14

Voice.

DataBridge speaks like a calm senior colleague — direct, never flattering, lightly editorial when it matters. The AI speaks with slightly more grace (serif italics, sentence fragments allowed) but never "Hi! ✨".

AI RESPONSE — DO

AI RESPONSE — DON'T

EMPTY STATE — DO

EMPTY STATE — DON'T

ERROR — DO

ERROR — DON'T
Error 500. Something went wrong. Please contact support.

AI presence.

You asked for subtle — so AI lives in a warm ochre counter-tone rather than a second loud color. AI-authored messages use serif for voice, the ai color for structural markers (avatar, left-border, confidence pill), and mono for technical foot-notes. No sparkles, ever.

DO
  • Use serif italics for the voice moment — one per message, at the start.
  • Show confidence + source tables in a mono footer — quiet but available.
  • Keep AI color (ochre) for AI surfaces only.
DON'T
  • No sparkle icons, no "✨", no purple gradients.

Charts.

14 / 14

The ten rules.

These are the non-negotiables. Hand them to Claude Code alongside the tokens. Every ambiguous call in Phase 4 and the handoff is resolved by pointing at one of these.

  1. One accent. Ocean is the only brand color. Every other color has a single semantic job.
  2. Serif for voice and numbers. Never for dense tables or chrome.
  3. Mono for technical truth. Column names, SQL, timestamps, row counts, coordinates.
  4. Lines, not glass. Thin 1px outlines replace glassmorphism, gradient meshes, and fake depth.
  5. Shadow is rare. Only modals, dropdowns, and toasts cast a shadow. Cards rest on lines.
  6. Tabular numerals always on KPI numbers, table cells, and chart annotations.
  7. AI has a color, not a mascot. Ochre for AI-authored surfaces — avatar, left border, confidence pill. Nothing else.
  8. No sparkles. No emoji. No purple gradients. Ever.
  9. Labels are mono, uppercase, 0.1em tracked. This is the product's signature rhythm.
  10. Empty states say something. "No dashboards yet." — short, serif, italic, then one sentence and one button.
